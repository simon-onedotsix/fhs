Real estate owns the building. Hospitality knows how to run it.

FHS Living is Future Hospitality Summit's platform for the hotelisation of real estate, across nine asset classes from branded residences and student housing to mixed use, hotels and resorts.

Nine asset classes. One room.

  1. Branded Residences Homes sold or let under a hotel or lifestyle brand, run to hospitality standards.
  2. Student Accommodation (PBSA) Purpose-built student homes, operated at scale with hotel-style services.
  3. Senior Living Independent and assisted living where care meets hospitality.
  4. Serviced Living Furnished apartments let by the night, month or year, fully serviced.
  5. Co-Living Private rooms with shared space and a programmed community.
  6. Shared Ownership Fractional and shared models that widen who can own.
  7. Club Concepts Members' clubs and residences built around community and access.
  8. Mixed Use Living, work and leisure in one place, often anchored by a hotel.
  9. Hotels & Resorts Hotels and resorts: the operating model the other eight classes learn from.
  • €62.2bn invested in European living real estate in 2025, up 22% on 2024. JLL 2026
  • €540m of European hotels bought by Middle Eastern investors in 2025, up 254% on 2024. HVS, 2025 European Hotel Transactions

The same building, operated.

This is the hotelisation of real estate.
